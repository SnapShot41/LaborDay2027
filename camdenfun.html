Explore the Area

Here are fun activities, attractions, and kid‑friendly places around Camden and Columbia. More will be added as we find them!

Top Family Activities (All Ages)

  • Riverbanks Zoo & Garden (Columbia) – Animals, splash pad, botanical garden, train ride.
  • Sesquicentennial State Park – Trails, playgrounds, canoe rentals, picnic areas.
  • Columbia Canal & Riverfront Park – Easy walking trail, great for photos.
  • Camden Revolutionary War Park – History + outdoor walking + kid‑friendly exhibits.
  • Bowlero Columbia – Bowling, arcade, food.

Kid-Friendly Activities (10 & Under)

  • EdVenture Children’s Museum – Hands‑on exhibits and play zones.
  • Camden Splash Pad – Free and perfect for hot days.
  • Saluda Shoals Park Splash Pad – Bigger splash area with more features.
  • Chuck E. Cheese (Columbia) – Easy fun for younger kids.
  • FunZone Skate Center – Roller skating + arcade.

Teen-Friendly Activities

  • Frankie’s Fun Park – Go‑karts, mini golf, arcade, laser tag.
  • Escape Plan Columbia – Escape rooms for groups.
  • AMC & Regal Cinemas – Movies for easy downtime.
  • Urban Air Adventure Park – Trampolines, climbing walls, ninja course.

Adult & Family Group Activities

  • Historic Camden – Guided tours and reenactments.
  • Camden Archives & Museum – Local history and artifacts.
  • Lake Wateree – Fishing, picnics, boat rentals.
  • Columbia Fireflies Baseball Game – Affordable and fun.
  • Congaree National Park – Boardwalk trail and stunning scenery.

Food & Treat Spots

  • Lizard’s Thicket – Southern comfort food.
  • Camden House of Pizza – Family favorite.
  • Sweet Frog – Frozen yogurt.
  • Blue Marlin – Seafood + Southern classics.
  • Rush’s – Burgers, shakes, kid‑friendly.
  • San Jose Mexican Restaurant – Great for groups.

Quick Activities (Short Visits)

  • Scoopy Doo Gelato – Ice cream stop.
  • Downtown Camden Walk – Shops and scenery.
  • Camden Battlefield – Quick historical stop.
  • Woodward Park – Playground + open space.
  • Camden Coffee House – Relax and recharge.
